Based on the methodology presented in this paper, it is proposed as future work:

\begin{itemize}
	\item As shown in Figure \ref{fig:TLBBC_m}, it is proposed to change the switching logic of the TLBBC converter to achieve not only two levels, but three voltage levels through LS-PWM modulation, which reduces the voltage on the power switches and increases their service life. Three-level switching can be interpreted as a vertical interleaving of the two bridges \cite{Eull}. This is considered essential to ensure that these power converters do not need to be replaced.
	\item Model the other internal elements of the hybrid powertrain to ultimately obtain a simulated model of the propulsion system that allows different load profiles to be tested.
	\item Based on the information provided in work, related to technical and economic aspects, the aim is to work on an optimization function that incorporates information from all these areas and allows us to identify the best battery alternatives, optimizing variables such as project cost, number of cells, available space, etc.
	\item The complexity of the battery bank model can be increased by adding dynamics that provide a better understanding of performance when a more detailed load profile is obtained, not only in terms of hours but also in terms of minutes or seconds.
	\item Implement PTO mode and analyze the feasibility of replacing diesel engines with ones with lower nominal power, allowing the use of batteries for towing tasks.
	\item In FreeSailing mode, the goal is to study how the genset can be used to charge the BESS and supply power to the propulsion system, with the aim of ensuring it operates at maximum efficiency (minimum fuel consumption).
	\item Consideration of port charger implementation, which could also impact the economic analysis of hybridization.
	\item Consider a DC main bus (rather than an AC bus) and analyze the technical and economic impact of the onboard charger and the controlled rectifier connected to the generator set.
	\item Through sensorization, monitor the health status of the different elements within the hybrid power system, especially the battery bank, in order to generate predictive maintenance routines and also have an operating history.
\end{itemize}
