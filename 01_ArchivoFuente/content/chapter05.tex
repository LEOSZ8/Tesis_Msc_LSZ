\subsection{Battery mathematical model}

One way to model the battery cell is by obtaining a mathematical model that allows us to determine the electrical performance of the cell.

To do this, the Shepherd mathematical model is used, which models the battery cell as a Thevenin equivalent, parameterizing the controlled voltage source, as shown in the following equation:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:Voc}
    V_{batt} = E_0 -K\frac{Q_0}{Q_0-Q}Q + A\cdot e^{-BQ}
\end{equation}

\begin{equation}
    Q = \int_0^t i_{bat}(t)dt
\end{equation}

Based on the parameters shown in equation X, the following parameters are described:

\begin{table}[H]
	\centering
	\caption{Technical input information.}
	\label{tab:Shepherd_zero}
	\begin{tabular}{|c|c|}
		\hline
		\multicolumn{2}{|c|}{\textbf{Input technical information}} \\
		\hline\hline
		$E_0$ & Open circuit voltage [V]    \\	
		\hline
		$K$ & Polarization voltage [V]   \\	
		\hline
		$Q_0$ & Nominal battery capacity [Ah]   \\	
		\hline
		$A$ & Exponential zone amplitude [V] \\	
		\hline
		$B$ & Exponential zone inverse time constant [1/Ah] \\	
		\hline
		\end{tabular}
\end{table} 


\begin{equation}\label{eq:ecuaciones}
\begin{split}
    A &= V_{full}-V_{exp}\\
    B &= \frac{3}{Q_{exp}}\\
    K &= \frac{(V_{full}-V_{nom}+A (e^{-B\cdot Q_{nom}}-1)) \cdot \Delta Q}{Q_{full}}\\
    E_0 &= V_{full} + K + R\cdot I_{nom} - A\\
    \Delta Q &=Q_{full}-Q_{nom}
\end{split}
\end{equation}
